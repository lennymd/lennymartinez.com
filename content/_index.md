_Hello!_ I'm a designer and PhD researcher at [ISIR](https://www.isir.upmc.fr/), Sorbonne Université. I research how generative AI systems are changing photographic practice, and how they affect our _agency_ to visually represent who we are and what we have lived and experienced. My PhD is supervised by [Baptiste Caramiaux](https://baptistecaramiaux.com/) and [Sarah Fdili Alaoui](https://saralaoui.com/), and is funded by [PEPR eNSEMBLE](https://www.pepr-ensemble.fr/en/).

I have a background in interaction design (MSc., TU Delft), photography & data visualization (MSc., Syracuse University) and mechanical engineering (BSc., MIT).

Previously, I taught data visualization and web development courses at the University of Miami. Before that, I've also been part of the teaching staff for MIT's [Product Engineering Process](https://2009.mit.edu/) course, as well as a [Carnegie-Knight News21](https://news21.com/about/) fellow investigating [hate crimes in America](https://hateinamerica.news21.com/).

In my free time, I boulder, go on long walks, and study new languages.
