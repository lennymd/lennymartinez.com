This project was a collaboration between the [University of Miami's School of Communication](https://com.miami.edu/) (Where I was teaching at the time), [MDB, Inc.](https://www.michaeldbaker.com/), and [RTI International](https://www.rti.org/), for the [Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute (PCORI)](https://www.pcori.org/).

As the lead developer, I worked with Alberto Cairo, Barbara Millet, and Gilberto Leon to design, develop, and evaluate an interactive visualization to summarize the findings of a systematic scoping review of studies on social needs interventions. You can see read more in the [project homepage](https://www.pcori.org/implementation-evidence/evidence-synthesis-reports-and-interactive-visualizations/evidence-maps-and-visualizations/social-needs-interventions-improve-health-outcomes).
