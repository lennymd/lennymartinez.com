As lead developer, I designed and built an interactive evidence map for a [PCORI](https://www.pcori.org/) scoping review of social needs interventions. Researchers can filter studies by need, population, design, quality, and setting, regroup them by population or outcome, open any study, and download the matching set as a list.

With Alberto Cairo and Barbara Millet at the [University of Miami](https://com.miami.edu/), and Gilberto Leon, and with [MDB, Inc.](https://www.michaeldbaker.com/), and [RTI International](https://www.rti.org/).
