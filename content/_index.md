**Hello!** I'm a designer and PhD researcher at [ISIR](https://www.isir.upmc.fr/), Sorbonne Université. I research how generative AI systems are changing photographic practice, and how they affect our _agency_ to visually represent who we are and what we have lived and experienced. My PhD is supervised by [Baptiste Caramiaux](https://baptistecaramiaux.com/) and [Sarah Fdili Alaoui](https://saralaoui.com/), and is funded by [PEPR eNSEMBLE](https://www.pepr-ensemble.fr/en/).

Previously, I studied interaction design at TU Delft, and taught data visualization and web development at the University of Miami.

In my free time, I boulder, and , and study new languages.
