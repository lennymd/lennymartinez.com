Prototyping Speculative AI was a week-long summer school held at [Politecnico
di Torino](https://www.polito.it/) in September 2024, bringing together
students to develop speculative design prototypes that interrogate how AI
systems classify and measure people. Rather than treating speculation as
free-form futuring, the program grounded it historically — participants worked
from the history of classification in art and design, and from Cesare
Lombroso's physiognomy, toward prototypes confronting how contemporary AI
inherits those logics.

The week combined lectures with hands-on prototyping. Maria Luce Lupetti
taught speculative design, Serena Cangiano the history of classification in
the arts, Andrea Di Salvo the history of Lombroso, Willem van der Maden the
translation of human experience into observable metrics for AI, and Alice Mela
the value of prototyping. Students moved through these methods across the week
and closed by building working prototypes of speculative AI projects.
