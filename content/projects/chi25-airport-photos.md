Airport Photos is a short story set in a country that has confiscated every private camera. A state-run generative model, MemoreVisium (MV), is now the only lawful source of images, and anyone who wants a picture types a description into an MV terminal and gets back a generated print. The narrator, a visitor leaving the country, uses the terminal at the airport to picture the best meal of their trip, then spends the rest of the wait trying to get it right. I wrote it to work through a tension from [an interview study with documentary photographers](/projects/chi25/).

Presented at the [HCI Design Stories](https://hcidesignstories.com/chi-2025/) workshop at CHI 2025, Yokohama. [Read the draft (PDF)](/files/chi25-airport-photos.pdf).
