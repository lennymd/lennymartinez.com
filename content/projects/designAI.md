As part of the [2023 Design and AI Symposium](https://www.tudelft.nl/io/actueel/congressen-en-symposia/designai-symposium-2023) at TU Delft, I participated in a workshop where we created a speculative copy of the Interactions Magazine exploring our fears and dreams around AI fever.

For this speculative magazine, I worked on a story about the effects of tampering with model abilities when they are widespread in society. The article was written by [Arganka Yahya](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arganka/) and myself, using ChatGPT for formatting and connecting details.
