Airport Photos is a short story about a country that does not allow individuals to make pictures with cameras. Instead, all visuals must be generated using a singular, government-approved image generation model. This short story was submitted to the [HCI Design Stories](https://hcidesignstories.com/) workshop at the CHI 2025 conference in Yokohama, Japan.
